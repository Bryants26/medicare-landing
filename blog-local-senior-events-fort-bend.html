Community Events

Free Medicare 101 Events in Fort Bend County

By Bryant Waldron, Licensed Independent Insurance Agent · Serving Richmond, Sugar Land & Fort Bend County, TX

Understanding Medicare is easier in a room with other people asking the same questions you have. That's the idea behind the free Medicare 101 events Bryant hosts around Fort Bend County — plain-language education, with no sales pitch and no Scope of Appointment required to attend.

What these events actually are

Each session covers the basics: the parts of Medicare, key enrollment periods, and the kinds of questions worth asking every year. It's a group setting, so you'll hear other attendees' questions too — often ones you hadn't thought to ask yourself.

These sessions are strictly educational. Per CMS guidelines, plan-specific sales conversations and enrollment don't happen at these events — that's a separate, individual conversation you can schedule afterward if you'd like one.

Where they're held

Sessions rotate through community locations across Fort Bend County, including area YMCA ForeverWell programs, senior centers, and community meal programs. Locations and dates change throughout the year, especially picking up frequency heading into the fall Annual Enrollment Period.

Who should attend

  • Anyone approaching 65 who wants to understand the basics before enrolling
  • Current Medicare beneficiaries who want a refresher before AEP
  • Family members helping a parent or loved one navigate Medicare decisions
  • Anyone who's simply confused by the mail and marketing that shows up around Medicare season

Want to know about the next one?

The easiest way to hear about upcoming sessions is to reach out directly — dates and locations are added throughout the year, and there's no cost or obligation to attend.
