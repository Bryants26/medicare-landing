When Is Medicare's Annual Enrollment Period? Key Dates & Deadlines

By Bryant Waldron, Licensed Independent Insurance Agent · Serving Richmond, Sugar Land & Fort Bend County, TX

If you have Medicare, one date range matters more than almost any other: the Annual Enrollment Period, often called AEP or "open enrollment." Every year, this is your main window to review your coverage and make changes for the year ahead.

The dates: October 15 – December 7

Medicare's Annual Enrollment Period runs from October 15 through December 7 every year, with no exceptions for weekends or holidays. Any change you make during this window takes effect on January 1 of the following year.

If you don't make any changes during AEP, your current coverage generally continues into the new year — but that doesn't mean it's still your best option. Plans, networks, and drug formularies can all change from one year to the next, even if you never touch anything.

What you can change during AEP

  • Switch from Original Medicare to a Medicare Advantage plan, or vice versa
  • Switch from one Medicare Advantage plan to another
  • Join, switch, or drop a Medicare Part D prescription drug plan

Why the fall matters even if you're happy with your plan

Every year, plans send out an "Annual Notice of Change" (ANOC) in September. It's worth reading, because it outlines exactly what's shifting for the coming year — premiums, copays, provider networks, and covered drugs can all move without much fanfare. A plan that fit perfectly last year isn't guaranteed to fit the same way next year.

A simple way to think about it

AEP isn't just for people who want to switch. It's an annual checkup for your coverage — a chance to confirm your doctors are still in-network, your medications are still covered at a price that works, and you're not missing out on extra benefits you're entitled to.
