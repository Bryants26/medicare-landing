# Turning 65 in Houston? Your Medicare Roadmap

If you're turning 65 and live in the Houston area — Richmond, Sugar Land, Katy, Missouri City, or anywhere across Fort Bend County — you're about to make a handful of decisions that will shape your healthcare coverage for years to come. The good news: you don't have to figure it out alone, and there's no cost to get help thinking it through.

Here's a plain-language walkthrough of what's coming and when.

## Your Enrollment Timeline

Your **Initial Enrollment Period (IEP)** is a 7-month window built around your 65th birthday:

- 3 months **before** your birthday month
- Your birthday month
- 3 months **after**

Enrolling at the right point in this window helps you avoid potential coverage gaps or late enrollment penalties later on. If you're still working and covered by an employer group health plan, your timeline may look different — that's worth confirming directly rather than guessing.

## What "Medicare" Actually Covers

Medicare isn't a single plan — it's a set of parts you combine based on your health needs and budget:

**Part A** covers hospital stays, skilled nursing, and hospice care.

**Part B** covers doctor visits, outpatient care, and preventive services.

Together, Parts A and B make up **Original Medicare** — but on their own, they leave gaps (copays, coinsurance, no prescription drug coverage). That's where the next two pieces come in.

**Medicare Supplement (Medigap)** plans pair with Original Medicare to help cover those out-of-pocket costs, generally letting you see any doctor nationwide who accepts Medicare.

**Medicare Advantage (Part C)** plans are a private alternative — they bundle hospital, medical, and often drug coverage into a single plan, usually through a network, and often include extra benefits like dental or vision.

**Part D** is prescription drug coverage — available on its own or built into most Medicare Advantage plans. It's worth having even if you're not taking medications yet, since going without it for too long can lead to a penalty later.

## A Simple Checklist Before You Decide

- Confirm your Medicare card or online account is set up
- Decide between Original Medicare + Medigap + Part D, or Medicare Advantage
- Check that your current doctors and prescriptions are covered under any plan you're considering
- Know your enrollment window so you don't miss it
- Ask questions before you commit — there's no cost to compare your options
